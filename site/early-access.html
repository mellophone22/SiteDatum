Windows Early Access

Distribution route approved — installer not yet published

Read this before purchasing

SiteDatum plans to distribute an unsigned Windows Early Access build. It will not carry a trusted publisher signature. Microsoft Defender SmartScreen may display an unknown-publisher warning, and Smart App Control, antivirus software, or an organization’s security policy may block installation completely without offering an override.

Keep your computer’s protections enabled

Do not disable Microsoft Defender, Smart App Control, antivirus protection, or an employer’s security policy to install SiteDatum. SiteDatum Support will not ask you to weaken those controls. A managed work computer may require approval from its administrator and may not be eligible for this Early Access channel.

Verify each download

Every available build will identify its SiteDatum version, source commit, publication date, Authenticode status, and SHA-256 checksum on this canonical website. Download only from a link published on sitedatum.site, then confirm the downloaded file matches that release information. Do not install copies received through email, chat, URL shorteners, mirrors, or file-sharing services.

Current availability

No installer is published yet. Purchase and download will remain closed until the final immutable candidate passes local and GitLab verification, its real checksum is published here, and the complete public download/install path is tested.

If Windows blocks installation

Keep the protection in place. Contact supportsitedatum@protonmail.com privately with the SiteDatum version, Windows version, and the exact warning text—without sending project documents, databases, credentials, payment-card information, or private company policy. If the device cannot safely install the unsigned build, use the published refund process.

Future signed distribution

This Early Access route is temporary. A trusted signed Windows distribution remains the goal for general availability.
