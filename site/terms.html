The product

SiteDatum is single-user, local-first Windows project engineering software. Users remain responsible for their project decisions, professional obligations, source documents, and backup practices.

Free and Pro

Free is intended to support three active projects and complete manual workflows. Pro is intended to allow unlimited active projects and provide workflow acceleration, advanced interchange, templates, bulk operations, and professional outputs.

Non-destructive expiration

Cancellation retains Pro access through the paid-through date. Expiration does not delete, hide, move, or make existing records uneditable. Backup, recovery, and an essential machine-readable export remain available.

Files and backups

Real project documents remain normal Windows files. SiteDatum does not replace appropriate document management, contractual recordkeeping, or independent backups.

Acceptable use

Users must not interfere with licensing or security controls, distribute malicious copies, use the software unlawfully, or claim that SiteDatum replaces professional judgment or contractual requirements.

Changes and discontinuation

Material product, pricing, and policy changes should be communicated before taking effect. A completed agreement must define notice, termination, and customer-remedy requirements.
