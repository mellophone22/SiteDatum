Cancellation and refunds

Founder/legal review draft — not yet effective

Paid subscriptions are not publicly available. This draft records intended product behavior and must be reconciled with Stripe Managed Payments terms, applicable law, publisher identity, and a private billing-support channel before launch.

Cancellation

Customers should be able to cancel through the hosted customer portal. Cancellation does not end Pro immediately; service remains Pro through the paid-through date.

After expiration

Existing projects and records remain visible and editable. SiteDatum never deletes or hides customer work because a subscription ends. Creating or restoring projects is limited by the Free active-project allowance after expiration.

Refund requests

A completed policy must define eligibility, request deadlines, exceptions, processing time, and the merchant-of-record workflow. Payment-card information must never be requested through SiteDatum support.

Effect of a refund

A completed policy must specify how refunded service periods affect entitlement while preserving the non-destructive data guarantees above.

Contact

A private billing-support address must be published before paid launch. The public GitLab issue tracker must not be used for billing disputes or payment information.
