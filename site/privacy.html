Privacy policy

Founder/legal review draft — not yet effective

This draft describes SiteDatum’s intended privacy boundary. It must be reviewed, completed with publisher contact information, and formally published before commercial launch.

Local project data

SiteDatum is designed so project records are stored in a local SQLite database and project documents remain ordinary Windows files. SiteDatum’s billing, licensing, and operational systems must not receive project names, tasks, RFIs, submittals, notes, contacts, file paths, documents, or database contents.

Accountless Free use

The Free edition is intended to work without registration. Ordinary local project work does not require a SiteDatum account.

Paid identity and licensing

If a customer purchases Pro, SiteDatum may process the minimum identity and billing references needed to create hosted checkout, recover paid entitlement, manage device activations, and open the merchant’s customer portal. Payment-card information is entered into Stripe-hosted surfaces and is not stored by the SiteDatum desktop application.

Support information

Information a user deliberately submits for support is processed to answer the request. Users must not send project documents, databases, secrets, payment-card information, or private customer data through public support channels.

Telemetry

No product analytics or privacy-safe operational telemetry is authorized for the initial release unless separately approved and disclosed before collection.

Retention and deletion

Local project data remains under the user’s control. Retention periods and deletion procedures for licensing and support records must be completed before this policy becomes effective.

Contact

Publisher privacy contact information must be added before launch.
